What is Bacteriostatic Water?

A laboratory-grade reconstitution solution made from deionized water with 0.9% benzyl alcohol. Designed for controlled laboratory, research, and industrial applications where a consistent aqueous solvent is required.

The benzyl alcohol maintains solution stability during routine laboratory handling and repeated access. Suitable for research settings requiring measured withdrawals over time.
